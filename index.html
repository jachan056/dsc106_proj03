About the data

What is CMIP6?

The Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 is a coordinated international effort to simulate past and future climates using numerous models. This project utlizes CESM2 from NCAR.

What is SSP5-8.5?

SSP5-8.5 is a high-emissions scenario where radiative forcing is projected to reach 8.5 W/m² by 2100. It is representative of a world where fossil fuels are heavily relied on with little to none climate policies.

Temperature Changes

Each value shows how much warmer or cooler a location is relative to its average during 1850–1900 (pre-industrial). Positive values indicate warming above the pre-industrial temperatures.

Data pipeline

Raw zarr data was loaded via Google Cloud, converted from Kelvin to Celsius, averaged annually, and sampled every 5 years. Regions were assigned by latitude threshold.

Regions in the dataset

Arctic / High North Latitude above 60° (including 60)
Northern Mid-Latitudes Latitude between 30° and 60° (not including 60)
Tropics Latitude between -30° and 30° (not including 30 and -30)
Southern Mid-Latitudes Latitude between -60° and 30° (not including -60)
Antarctic / High South Latitude below -60° (including -60)
